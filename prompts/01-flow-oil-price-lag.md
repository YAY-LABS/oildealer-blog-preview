Create a clean Korean editorial hero illustration for a B2B blog article aimed at fuel-station owners.

ARTICLE TITLE:
유가 하락 뉴스가 내 발주 단가까지 오기 전에 생기는 일

PURPOSE:
Show that international oil price news does not become a gas station owner's actual order price immediately. It passes through several layers: exchange rate, refining/product margin, domestic supply price, dealer terms, and inventory average cost.

VISUAL STRUCTURE:
- Wide 16:9 canvas
- Warm white / very light cream background
- Centered left-to-right mechanism flow
- Use thin rounded cards connected by subtle arrows
- Main flow cards:
  1. 국제유가 뉴스
  2. 환율
  3. 정제마진
  4. 국내 공급가
  5. 내 발주 단가
- Add a smaller lower card connected into the final step:
  재고 평균단가
- Show small friction/delay marks between steps, like subtle dotted intervals or small clock marks
- Add one small note label: 바로 반영되지 않음

STYLE:
- Minimal editorial vector illustration
- Calm analytical briefing style
- No photorealism
- No 3D render
- No cartoon mascot
- No stock photo people
- No oil barrel cliché
- No gas pump cliché
- No flashy fintech gradient
- High-end Korean SaaS blog feel
- Similar restraint to a clean clinical/knowledge blog, but using OilDealer purple accents

COLORS:
- Background: #fffdfa or #ffffff
- Primary accent: #721fe5
- Soft purple fills: #f5f0ff
- Lines: #e4d7ff
- Main text: #15151a
- Muted text: #737782
- Use purple sparingly, mostly for arrows and emphasis

TYPOGRAPHY IN IMAGE:
Use Korean labels only. Keep text minimal and large enough to read. Avoid long sentences.

COMPOSITION:
Generous negative space. The image should sit well above a long-form article title. It should look like a quiet market memo diagram, not an advertisement.
